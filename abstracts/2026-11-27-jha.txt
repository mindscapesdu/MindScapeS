TALK TITLE: Nyaya Epistemology: Sabda-Pramana and the Cognitive Structure of Understanding
SPEAKER: Prof. V. N. Jha (University of Pune / Chinmaya Vishwavidyapeeth)
DATE: Friday, November 27, 2026 • 6:30 PM IST

ABSTRACT:
In classical Indian epistemology, the Nyaya school developed a rigorously articulated theory of cognition (jnana) and valid knowledge sources (pramana), with particular depth accorded to verbal testimony (sabda-pramana).

This presentation investigates the conditions under which an utterance generates immediate cognitive grasp in a hearer (sabda-bodha). According to the Navya-Nyaya framework, linguistic comprehension requires the mutual satisfaction of four epistemic conditions: expectancy (akanksa), semantic compatibility (yogyata), contiguity (sannidhi), and knowledge of speaker intention (tatparya-jnana). We will analyze the formal logic through which Nyaya philosophers model these relations and evaluate their striking convergence with contemporary debates in the philosophy of language regarding contextualism, testimony, and cognitive pragmatics.
