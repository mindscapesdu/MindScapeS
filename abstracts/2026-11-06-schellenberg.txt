TALK TITLE: Perceptual Capacities and the Grounds of Mental Evidence
SPEAKER: Prof. Susanna Schellenberg (Rutgers University)
DATE: Friday, November 6, 2026 • 8:00 AM IST

ABSTRACT:
Perceptual experiences provide both conscious phenomenology and rational justification for empirical beliefs. How can a sensory state possess phenomenal force while simultaneously providing epistemic warrant?

In this talk, I defend a capacitive framework of perceptual experience. Perception, on this view, fundamentally involves the exercise of repeatable mental capacities aimed at discriminating and singling out particular objects, properties, and events in the environment. Employing these capacities under favorable conditions yields factive perceptual evidence. When capacities misfire due to illusion or hallucination, they nevertheless retain derivative justificatory power because they remain tokens of a fundamentally discriminative cognitive capacity. I will trace the consequences of this view for internalist versus externalist accounts of epistemic warrant.
