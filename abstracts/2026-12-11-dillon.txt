TALK TITLE: Visual Intuitions, Geometry, and Symbolic Form in Cognitive Development
SPEAKER: Prof. Moira Dillon (New York University)
DATE: Friday, December 11, 2026 • 6:30 PM IST

ABSTRACT:
Human beings possess a distinctive capacity to perceive geometric relationships, generate visual art, and translate spatial intuitions into symbolic diagrams. What cognitive architecture underlies these abilities, and how do they emerge during childhood?

This presentation examines developmental studies exploring children's intuitive geometry and spontaneous drawing behaviors. I discuss findings showing how children utilize geometric properties such as angle, length, and shape across varied communicative tasks. We contrast core visual spatial mechanisms with formal symbolic systems (such as Euclidean geometry and cartography), exploring how intuitive visual schemas are transformed into formal epistemic tools.
