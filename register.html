Code of Conduct for Discussions

MindScapeS is dedicated to providing a collegial, serious, and welcoming environment for philosophical debate. We ask all participants to abide by three basic practices:

  1. Respectful discourse: All critiques must be substantive, constructive, and aimed at the arguments presented rather than individuals.
  2. Focus: Comments and questions should directly engage the specific conceptual, empirical, or methodological arguments of the paper.
  3. Student questions first: Following each talk, the first round of questioning is reserved exclusively for undergraduate and graduate students. Faculty and senior researchers are invited to participate during the commentators' response and open Q&A.
